GIS Portfolio Lab

Analytical GIS products built from open data. The analysis runs locally; each app below serves the precomputed result and says what the evidence can and cannot support. More apps will be added here.

  • Parcel Intelligence — Amherst, MA

    How much of each parcel lies outside mapped constraints, and how much of that depends on the assumptions?

    Status: potential feasibility under stated screening assumptions, not a development entitlement.

    Open Parcel Intelligence (Amherst, MA)

    Map: MassGIS (Bureau of Geographic Information), FEMA and USGS data; analysis: this project.

  • Landslide Susceptibility — Buncombe & Yancey, NC

    Which hillslopes are most susceptible to rainfall-triggered landslide initiation, and how well did that ranking match Hurricane Helene?

    Status: local experiment. A relative ranking that holds inside these two counties; it does not transfer beyond them and is not a hazard or risk map.

    Open Landslide Susceptibility (Buncombe and Yancey, NC)

    Image: NC OneMap Orthoimagery Helene 2025 (NC Collaboratory at UNC Chapel Hill); NC OneMap (2019), www.nconemap.gov. Ranking overlay: this project.
