# M4 baseline and spatial validation

Generated by `make baseline` from `validation/m4_baseline.json`. Scores are **relative susceptibility ranks**, not probabilities of future landslides. Labels are *mapped* Helene initiation sites; an unmapped slope is not a proven stable slope.

## Design

- Slope units A = 0.1 km², 22,569 units ≥ 50 % in the triggered domain (MRMS 72 h ≥ 250 mm); 5 m hillslope cells as the second unit.
- Leave-one-HUC-12-out; training rows within 500 m of the held-out watershed dropped.
- Rainfall is an adjustment covariate; the score is the prediction at the triggered-domain median rainfall (334 mm) and median unit area (0.066 km²).
- Primary metric: AUC within 50 mm rainfall bins, weighted by positives. Within-watershed AUC is the strictest view (no between-watershed contrast at all). Pooled AUC is a contrast.
- Intervals: 95 % HUC-12 block bootstrap (2000 resamples). Effective n ≈ the number of watersheds with positives, so intervals are wide.
- No external reference product was scored: the USGS national map (ScienceBase) was not obtained (optional, scripted download blocked) and the NCGS debris-flow model was published after Helene. The rule references below are comparisons, not validation.

## Decision gates (fixed before the first fit)

- **G1** rainfall-stratified AUC >= 0.7, lower >= 0.6: 0.767 (0.704–0.814) → **pass**
- **G2** AUC > 0.6 in >= 7 of the replicates: 9 of 9 → **pass**
- Proceed to M5: **yes**

- **Calibration (diagnostic only, not shown in the app):** Brier 0.0494 vs climatology 0.0518 (skill 0.047). This is fit to one event's mapped inventory, so it is not a probability of a future landslide.
