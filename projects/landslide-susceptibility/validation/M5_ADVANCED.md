Generated by `make advanced` from `validation/m5_advanced.json`. Scores are **relative susceptibility ranks**, not probabilities of future landslides; labels are *mapped* Helene initiation sites.

Design as M4 (leave-one-HUC-12-out, 500 m buffer, score at the reference rainfall 334 mm). Gradient boosting is tuned inside each training fold only (inner CV grouped by HUC-12: 3 folds for units, 2 for cells; metric rainfall-stratified AUC). Criteria and the product rule were fixed before the first M5 fit (METHOD §Validation).

- **Product (highest within-watershed AUC among C2 passes):** **none: stop for an owner decision**. Selecting among four candidates on the same out-of-fold results is mildly optimistic.
