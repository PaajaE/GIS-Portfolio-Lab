**Post-hoc diagnosis (explanation only, no model changed):**
- AOI natural initiations lie only in geology groups ['1', '3']; 80.1 % of the Watauga test cells are in group 2.
- Transferred model without its geology terms: AUC 0.702 (with them 0.500).
- AOI hillslopes by geology group (share of area, median 72 h rainfall, positive cells): 1: 86.3 %, 350 mm, 12,499; 2: 6.0 %, 268 mm, 0; 3: 6.9 %, 535 mm, 3,757; 4: 0.8 %, 267 mm, 0.
- The rock types without positives are the least rained, so the geology terms largely encode the rainfall gradient. That is a confound in the product, which the within-AOI validation cannot detect.
