How the ranking was made and checked

  • Target: natural initiation zones (source areas) of Helene landslides mapped by the NC Geological Survey on 2025 imagery and lidar change detection, excluding road-, cut- or drainage-related failures.
  • Terrain: 5 m predictors from 2016–17 USGS 3DEP lidar (slope, curvature, relief, position, roughness, contributing area, aspect), plus land cover, canopy, geology and soils.
  • Trigger: only land that received at least … mm in 72 h (MRMS radar) is ranked; rainfall is held at the median (… mm), so the map ranks terrain under equal rain, not where it rained most.
  • Model: logistic regression on 5 m cells. Gradient boosting was ….
  • Check: leave-one-watershed-out (HUC-12, 500 m buffer). Ranking quality within watersheds: model AUC …, slope alone …. Only … watersheds have enough mapped sites to be scored on their own, so intervals are wide.
  • Slope units: aggregated to small hillslope units, the model is not detectably better than slope alone on the primary metric; this prototype therefore shows the 5 m ranking.

What this screen cannot tell you

  • Whether a landslide will happen. The ranking is relative and was learned from one storm's mapped inventory. It is not a probability, a recurrence interval or a hazard.
  • Where debris goes. Only initiation is ranked; runout paths and the areas they reach are not modelled.
  • Road, cut and fill failures. They were excluded from the target; near roads, actual failures are mostly of that kind.
  • That an unflagged or unmapped slope is safe. A flagged slope without a mapped Helene initiation is "high susceptibility, no mapped initiation", not a model error: Helene did not test every slope equally and not every failure was mapped. Some well-watered watersheds have almost no mapped failures of any kind; whether that reflects lower rainfall intensity or unmapped terrain cannot be told from these data.
  • Other places, storms or terrain changes. It was built and checked inside one storm footprint in two counties; terrain graded after 2017 is not represented. Trained on one of the two counties it ranks the other worse than slope alone, and in neighbouring Watauga County it ranked Helene initiation sites no better than chance. Treat it as a local ranking.
  • Whether rock type matters. The model's geology terms largely mirror the rainfall gradient: the rock types that received the least rain have no mapped natural initiations. Rankings across geology boundaries are therefore uncertain; this is being reviewed.
  • Fine detail at the finest zoom. The map shows the 5 m ranking generalised to about 8 m pixels.
